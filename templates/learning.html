How to read this
Edge is zero when a signal carries no information: a return net of its benchmark, a directional call net of the market's average day, or a hit rate net of what you would get without the signal. The dot is the estimate; the line is its {{ L.method.ci }} interval. An interval that crosses zero has not shown an edge.
Sessions, not rows, are the evidence. Two hundred setups screened on one close move together, so they count as one date. A 10-day outcome logged today shares nine days with tomorrow's, so "Indep. windows" counts the non-overlapping ones.
Judged together. Cohorts in one table are corrected as a group (false-discovery rate {{ L.method.fdr }}), so one lucky split among many does not earn a verdict.
Accumulating means fewer than {{ L.method.min_obs }} observations, {{ L.method.min_dates }} sessions or {{ L.method.min_windows }} independent windows. A hollow dot is an estimate that has not been judged yet.
